simgpu is deployed.

Check simulated GPU resources registered on each node:
  kubectl describe nodes | grep -A5 "simgpu.dev/gpu"

Watch the sample trainer job get scheduled and simulate training steps:
  kubectl get pods -l app=simgpu-trainer
  kubectl logs -f job/simgpu-trainer

Reach the API:
  kubectl port-forward svc/simgpu-api 8000:8000

{{- if .Values.web.enabled }}
Reach the website (the trainer Job reports its progress here live, under
"Live training run" — note the website's browser-side calls need
NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL baked in at image build time, so also port-forward the API):
  kubectl port-forward svc/simgpu-web 3000:3000
  kubectl port-forward svc/simgpu-api 8000:8000
